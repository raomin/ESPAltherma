ESPAltherma

Monitor your Daikin Altherma heat pump in Home Assistant. Install from your browser: no software, no configuration file.

1Choose your board

2Install

Plug the board into this computer with a USB data cable, then click Install. After the installation, the installer asks for your WiFi network.

Your browser cannot talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. The installer needs a secure page (https or localhost).

The board does not show up? Install the USB driver of your board (CP210x or CH340), and try another cable: some only charge.

3Connect it to the heat pump

Power the heat pump off first. Find the X10A connector on the main board of the indoor unit (with a Bi-zone kit, use X12A on the Bi-zone board: same pins).

X10ABoard
1 · 5V5V / VIN (can power the board)
2 · TXGPIO 16 (RX)
3 · RXGPIO 17 (TX)
5 · GNDGND (always connect it, even when powered by USB)

The pins can be changed later in the web interface (Settings). ROTEX units with an 8-pin X10A: see the wiring guide.

4Finish in the web interface

  1. Open http://espaltherma.local (or the address shown by the installer).
  2. ESPAltherma reads your heat pump and detects its model. Confirm it.
  3. Click Find my Home Assistant to connect it to your MQTT broker. The sensors appear in Home Assistant automatically.

No WiFi entered, or the network changed? The board opens a setup network ESPAltherma-XXXX (password espaltherma): join it and follow the page that opens.
