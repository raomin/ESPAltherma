Step 2 · Home Assistant

Connect to Home Assistant

ESPAltherma sends its values to Home Assistant through an MQTT broker, usually the Mosquitto add-on. Home Assistant then creates the sensors automatically.

Tip: in Home Assistant, create a dedicated user for ESPAltherma (Settings → People) and use it here.

Diagnostics

 
Definition corrections

  
Some definitions do not match every unit: a value is then read across two neighbours and shows absurd, jumping numbers. ESPAltherma checks the replies and reads such a registry with the layout of the definition that fits.

  

  
  

 
Event history

  
Restarts and their causes, network and MQTT changes, supply drops, firmware updates. Kept across restarts; after a power cut, the last two minutes may be missing.

  

  

 
Heat pump survey

  
Every registry of the heat pump, read once, with its identification codes and the detection result.
